Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how [PhantomNWA] uses cookies and similar technologies (including browser local storage) on this website (the "Site").

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Local storage is a similar browser technology that lets a site save data on your device, but it is not automatically sent to a server with every request the way cookies are.

2. What We Use & Why

TechnologyPurposeProvider
Browser local storageRemembers items in your shopping cart between visits, on your device onlyFirst-party (this Site)
Analytics cookiesMeasures site traffic, page views, and visitor behavior to help us improve the SiteGoogle Analytics
Checkout session dataEnables secure payment processing during checkoutSquare, Inc.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited, time on page, device/browser type, and approximate location (derived from IP address). This information is processed by Google in accordance with its Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Local Storage (Shopping Cart)

To let you build a cart without creating an account, we store your selected items directly in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device — it is not transmitted to or stored on our servers unless and until you complete checkout through Square. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data/cache.

5. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Note that blocking certain cookies (like analytics cookies) generally won't break site functionality, but clearing local storage will empty your shopping cart.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Square's checkout process may set its own cookies to enable secure payment processing. These are governed by Square's Privacy Policy, not this one.
