1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. If you fill out a form on our Site (for example, joining our community list) or contact us, we collect the information you submit, such as your name and email address.

Purchase information. When you make a purchase, checkout is processed by Square, a third-party payment processor. We may receive limited order information from Square (such as your name, shipping address, email, and purchase details) to fulfill your order.

Shopping cart data. Items you add to your cart before checkout are stored using your browser's local storage, on your own device. This cart data is not automatically transmitted to or stored on our servers, and it stays on your device until you clear your browser data or complete checkout.

Automatically collected information. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring website, typically through cookies or similar technologies. This data is aggregated and used to help us improve the Site.

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and browser local storage for the following purposes:

  • Necessary/functional: local storage to remember items in your shopping cart.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies to measure site traffic and usage patterns.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can clear local storage at any time by clearing your browser's site data. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.
